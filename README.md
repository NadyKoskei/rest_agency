# Noir-Vi Sulwe — A Cozy Dinner on the Web

Welcome to the tiny digital home of Noir-Vi Sulwe — a fictional little restaurant where the flavors are bold, the lights are low, and every dish tells a story.

This repo holds a small static website: a warm hero image, a simple menu gallery, and room for your own photos and copy. It's perfect for mockups, practice, or to serve as the starting plate for a real site.

## What you'll find

- `public/index.html` — the page visitors see.
- `public/assets/css/style.css` — the stylesheet that dresses the site.
- `public/assets/images/` — hero and menu images (swap these for your own photography).

## What you can play with

- Change the hero photo to set the mood — sunrise, candlelight, or rainy-window cozy.
- Swap the menu images and captions to showcase your best dishes.
- Tweak colors and spacing in `style.css` for a custom vibe.

## Design notes (friendly, not technical)

- The hero is meant to be dramatic: a wide image with a soft overlay so text reads easily.
- Menu items are presented in a clean row — think of them like three plates on a table.
- The footer sits quietly at the bottom so guests leave with a satisfied smile.

## Want help making it sing?

- I can add a centered headline and call-to-action on the hero (e.g., "Reserve a Table").
- I can add simple hover effects for the menu photos so they feel interactive.
